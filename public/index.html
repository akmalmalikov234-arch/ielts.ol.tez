Learning with short daily sessions

Many learners try to study for several hours at once. A shorter daily routine can be easier to maintain. Ten or twenty focused minutes can help a learner build a habit and notice progress.

The most useful routine is one that can be repeated. A learner can read a short article, write five new words, and then use those words in two sentences.
